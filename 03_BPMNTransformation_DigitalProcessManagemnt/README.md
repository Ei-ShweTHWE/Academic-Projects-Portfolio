# 03 - Digital Process Management: Volkswagen Transformation

## 📌 Project Overview
A master's coursework individual project focusing on digital process management, strategic transformation, and BPMN process modeling for Volkswagen's shift toward Electric Vehicle (EV) leadership.

## 🛠️ Strategic & Process Analysis
* **Transition Goals:** Analyzed Volkswagen's EV transition goals, including targeting 70% EV sales in Europe and achieving carbon neutrality.
* **Core Components:** Evaluated key components such as the MEB platform, integrated supply chain, real-time analytics, and sustainability initiatives.

## 🔄 BPMN Process Modeling
* **Workflow Design:** Designed a comprehensive multi-pool BPMN workflow mapping end-to-end EV operations across Procurement, Production, Quality Control, and Logistics.
* **Advanced Integration:** Incorporated digital twins, battery assembly, and sustainability assessments into the process models.

## 🎯 Challenges & Key Achievements
* **Production Challenges:** Addressed critical EV production hurdles such as battery costs and workforce reskilling/upskilling.
* **Milestones:** Highlighted major milestones including successful ID.3/ID.4 launches and carbon-neutral manufacturing.
